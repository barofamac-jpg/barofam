Drop Bingo does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

We collect no information at all. The app has no sign-up, no login, and no user accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or any other personal detail.

The app requests no device permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, storage, location, or contacts.

We do not share data with anyone, because we hold none. The app currently contains no advertising and no analytics tools.

Drop Bingo is suitable for all ages. Since we collect no information from anyone, we also collect none from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If a future version of the app adds features that handle data — such as advertising or usage statistics — we will update this policy before releasing that version, and change the effective date above.
